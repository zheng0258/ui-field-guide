AI prompt

Build a Android Bottom sheet (ModalBottomSheet or BottomSheetDialogFragment). Also known as: Material modal bottom sheet, half-height sheet. It should have: drag handle on top; covers the lower half; scrim behind. Do not build a Modal dialog.
