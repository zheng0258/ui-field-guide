AI prompt

Build a Android Top app bar (TopAppBar / CenterAlignedTopAppBar or MaterialToolbar). Also known as: iOS navigation bar, title with back button. It should have: page title; back button on the leading edge; action icons on the trailing edge. Do not build a Bottom navigation.
