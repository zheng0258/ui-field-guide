AI prompt

Build a Web Resizable panes (flex panes + focusable divider or separator with aria-valuenow (window splitter)). Also known as: NSSplitView, resizable pane divider. It should have: two or three panes side by side; draggable divider between panes; arrow keys move a focused divider. Do not build a Sidebar.
