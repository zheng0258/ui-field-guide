AI prompt

Build a iOS Navigation bar (UINavigationBar or .navigationTitle + .toolbar). Also known as: iOS navigation bar, title with back button. It should have: page title; back button on the leading edge; action icons on the trailing edge. Do not build a Bottom navigation.
