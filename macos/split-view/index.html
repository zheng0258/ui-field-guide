AI prompt

Build a macOS Split view (NSSplitView / NSSplitViewController or HSplitView / NavigationSplitView). Also known as: NSSplitView, resizable pane divider. It should have: two or three panes side by side; draggable divider between panes; arrow keys move a focused divider. Do not build a Sidebar.
